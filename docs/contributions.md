# Contributions

## My main work

I contributed to the product concept and designed the overall appearance and enclosure concept. I created the functional structure block diagram and wrote the full machine workflow explanation.

I developed the mechanical CAD for the conveyor and drive integration, rotating serving platform, top and bottom heating assemblies, eggshell collection and compression assembly, oil tank, and complete display casing and mounting assembly.

For every subsystem I developed, I selected the components and performed its functional calculations, such as speed, transmission, load, and drive sizing where applicable. I selected materials and independently prepared the manufacturing-technology documentation, final BOM, and technical assembly drawings.

## Shared and supporting work

I contributed to heating calculations, supported my teammate with the tunnel and solenoid mechanisms, and contributed to the D-FMEA. The D-FMEA was mainly my teammates' work.

## Teammates and commercial components

My teammate produced the freehand concept sketch and implemented the main enclosure and structural frame in CAD. He led the gate, tunnel, and solenoid mechanisms, with my support on their development and integration.

Other team contributions remain part of the full report, including the wider electrical, risk, planning, and cost work. The final BOM and manufacturing-technology documentation are my contributions; the separate industrial production and cost-planning chapters are not assigned to me here.

Bought motors, bearings, sensors, the display module, and other commercial parts are distinct from my custom part designs. Imported CAD geometry and simplified bought-part placeholders support integration; they are not presented as original component designs.

The full report retains its original team and source credits. No blanket redistribution licence has been added to the university drawing, the team report, or third-party component models.
