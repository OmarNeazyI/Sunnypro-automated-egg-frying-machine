# Materials and manufacturing technologies

I selected the materials and independently prepared the manufacturing-technology documentation. These are proposed production routes; none of the parts were manufactured.

[Read the original material choices and manufacturing descriptions](manufacturing/SunnyPro_Materials_and_Manufacturing.pdf) - report pages 162-206.

## Decision criteria

The choices consider thermal conductivity, heat exposure, corrosion resistance, cleanability, stiffness, mass, shape, locating features, and the assembly method. The documentation addresses both custom parts and the interfaces to purchased components.

| Part family | Documented selection and rationale | Proposed route |
|---|---|---|
| Cooking pocket plates | The manufacturing section specifies EN 1.4409 cast stainless steel for the pocket geometry and corrosion resistance | Precision casting, finishing, machining of interfaces, and polishing |
| Lower heating block | EN AW-6082 T651 aluminium for heat spreading and machinability | Sawing, CNC face/profile machining, heater bores, sensor bore, and deburring |
| Drawer, shielding, and frame parts | EN 1.4301 stainless steel for relevant heat, moisture, and cleaning exposure | Sheet cutting, bending where needed, TIG welding, deburring, and finishing |
| Rotating plate and support parts | Aluminium grades are selected in the manufacturing section to reduce supported mass | Turning, milling, drilling, countersinking, and anodising as specified |
| Selected motor housings | Cast aluminium with machined mounting and locating interfaces | Casting, reference-face machining, boring, drilling, and threading |
| Display housing | ABS-HI with insert features where specified | Proposed injection moulding, finishing, and insert installation |
| Main external case | ABS-HI sheet with a purchased glass insert | Routed panel features, joining, finishing, and window integration |

## Manufacturing detail

The source descriptions specify machining setups, reference surfaces, hole-making directions, bend lines, weld locations, finishing, and assembly interfaces. For example, the heating-block route separates top/outer machining, bottom reference machining, heater bores, and a temperature-sensor bore.

The source is an engineering production proposal. Process capability, tooling, tolerances, casting or moulding feasibility, material certificates, and suitability for actual food-service use have not been validated.

## Material consistency

The conveyor mass calculation assigns AISI 316 to custom moving parts, while the manufacturing selection specifies EN 1.4409 for the cooking pockets. The rotating plate is described inconsistently as stainless steel and aluminium in the report, while the manufacturing section specifies clear-anodised EN AW-6061 T6.

These entries need reconciliation before a manufacturing release. The source PDFs retain them so the selected grade, density used for calculations, surface treatment, and finished geometry can be checked together.
