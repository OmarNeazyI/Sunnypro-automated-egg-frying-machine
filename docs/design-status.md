# Design status and limitations

## Completed design work

The project produced a product concept, functional structure and workflow, CAD assemblies, component selection, functional calculations, materials and manufacturing proposals, a final BOM, and technical assembly documentation.

I developed the conveyor, rotating serving platform, top and bottom heating assemblies, eggshell drawer and compression mechanism, oil tank, display casing and mounting assembly, and overall appearance. I also prepared the functional diagram and workflow, final BOM, materials/manufacturing documentation, and technical assembly drawing.

I supported my teammate's tunnel and solenoid mechanisms and contributed to the team's D-FMEA. The full report includes other contributors' work.

## Evidence boundary

No part was manufactured or physically tested. The moving assemblies were developed for their intended CAD movement, as reported during design work. The complete conveyor assembly is demanding to run in motion studies. No completed dynamic simulation, physical functional test, food trial, or measured performance is claimed.

## Remaining work before manufacture or operation

| Area | Remaining work |
|---|---|
| Fasteners and interfaces | Correct known hole/thread mismatches; check clearances, access, fits, and locating features |
| Motion | Check running torque, friction, gearbox losses, chain tension, backlash, friction-drive slip, and reference-sensor repeatability |
| Timing | Reconcile the cooking-model speed, conveyor drive-sizing speed, indexed dwell, and serving-platform sequence |
| Materials | Reconcile conflicting descriptions and calculation densities; confirm grades and finishes |
| Thermal design | Validate heat transfer, contact conditions, temperature distribution, guarding, wiring exposure, and protection |
| Hygiene and food handling | Validate cleaning access, residue traps, food-contact materials, oil handling, shell separation, and safe operation |
| Safety and control | Implement and test interlocks, fault handling, heater cutoffs, and the complete operating sequence |
| Manufacturing | Validate the proposed processes and produce checked individual part drawings and specifications |

The requirements and figures in the original report are design targets or calculations. The original report also contains broad statements about performance and compliance; no certification or demonstrated compliance is claimed by this portfolio.
